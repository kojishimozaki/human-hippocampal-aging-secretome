# Calibration sources for the manuscript's headline numbers

These three files back numbers the manuscript states but that had no source inside this
repository. The 2026-08-26 audit raised that as BLOCKERs B-13 and B-14: the calibration
defending the 60-hit result, and the evidence behind the replication-to-reproduction
downgrade, existed only inside an audit run directory outside the repo.

**Provenance.** All three were produced by the third-party audit run `20260821-225834`
(audited commit `b6ab3b5771026506d3ea47b055fe45729f970ebc`) and copied here unchanged on
2026-08-27. They are *not* regenerated by any target in this repository — the scripts that
produced them live in the audit kit, not here. That limitation is stated rather than papered
over; see `audit_log/2026-08-27_provenance_b12_b13_b14/`.

| File | Produced by | Backs |
|---|---|---|
| `permutation_null_distribution.tsv` | audit kit `test_permutation.py` (100 donor-label permutations, full pipeline refit per permutation) | Results: the per-cell-type empirical p-values (0.020 microglia, 0.040 astrocytes, 0.040 vascular, 0.049 OPCs, 0.109 oligodendrocytes) and "the null median is zero in every cell type"; Limitation 1: the complete-null rates (29–42% for one or more BH-significant genes and up to 16% for ten or more in the primary cohort, 29–51% and 17% in GSE278576) and the largest null draws (1,331 and 1,928 genes) |
| `matched_background_replication.csv` | audit kit `pass5_01_matched_background_replication.py` (20,000 matched draws, seed 42) | Results: metric (i) p = 0.146 and metric (iii) p = 0.187 at k = 10 with \|log2FC\|+expression matching, and their recovery to p ≤ 0.024 at k ≥ 100 — the basis for describing the second cohort as reproduction rather than replication |
| `donorlabel_permutation_replication.csv` | audit kit `pass5_04_donorlabel_permutation_replication.py` (100 permutations, pyDESeq2 refit each time) | Results: all three replication metrics beyond a donor-label permutation null (mean signed p = 0.0099, sign-concordance p = 0.0297, pooled rho p = 0.0099) |

**Re-derivation check (2026-08-27).** Every manuscript number listed above was recomputed from
these files and matched: the null median is 0.0 in all five cell types, the complete-null rates
come out at 29–42% / 16% for GSE268609 and 29–51% / 17% for GSE278576, and the matched-background
metrics come out at 0.146 and 0.187.
